- `Resolution` and the target, node, TLS and auth types inside it: what `config` resolves to, `redis-connect` turns into a client and `redis-client` connects with, so none of them converts another's copy. A host builds its redis-py client from the same `Resolution`, reattaching `passthrough` and `host_objects`
- Data only. It depends on `secret-value`, `circuit-breaker` (for its dependency-free `Config`), `serde_json` and `veil` and nothing else, so `config` never pulls in redis-rs or tokio. Anything that needs redis-rs, like building a `ConnectionInfo` or reading TLS files, lives in `redis-client`
