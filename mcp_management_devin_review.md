F6 (P1) Admission for the management endpoint must be a separate minimal auth path, not MCPRequestHandler.process_mcp_request. That function returns upstream auth headers, OAuth headers, requested server lists and raw headers, and has anonymous admission branches for passthrough and DCR plus /.well-known/ public routing. None of that is wanted, and inheriting it means a future DCR anonymity change silently changes management admission.
